# 5.5.87 — IA collection depth and rotation repair

## Delivery scope

This release deepens the two enrolled desktop stations: Classic Rerun TV (10)
and Classic Cartoons (150). It does not claim the entire IA migration is finished
or enroll additional mobile/Cast stations. The shared strict-file qualification
repairs are covered by regression tests; unrelated provider adapters are unchanged.

## What was shallow

- Classic TV lacked explicit complete-series rails. Archive upload dates also
  obscured original broadcast years: the Benson collection is dated 2023 in
  Archive metadata, but its original series ran from 1979–1986.
- Classic Cartoons had only three recovery families.
- Background collection expansion stopped when search produced no next page,
  even if known series parents still had unprocessed episode files.
- Qualification could let one fast collection monopolize the ready shelf.
- A parent could resolve to an already watched first episode, bypassing the
  pre-hydration exclusion. Parent plus child could occupy duplicate ready slots.
- Underfill recovery discarded learned collection hints.
- Foreground hydration waited for unrelated slow metadata after a complete
  verified shelf was already available.

## Repairs

- Added metadata-inspected full-episode starter files and expandable parents for
  nine Classic TV families: Leave It to Beaver, Benson, Dick Van Dyke, Mary Tyler
  Moore, The Munsters, The Honeymooners, The Addams Family, Get Smart, Green Acres.
- Expanded cartoons to eight families: Clutch Cargo, Space Angel, Top Cat,
  Pink Panther Show, New Scooby-Doo Movies, Flintstones, Scooby-Doo Show, Jetsons.
- Preserved original series years, exact selected-file identities, deeper
  candidates and learned metadata. Metadata evidence is not called transport
  verification; actual ready admission still probes the media transport.
- Continued bounded parent harvesting independently of empty search pages.
- Balanced ready admission across series, with same-series unseen recovery only
  after alternative families fail. Watched-item exclusions remain enforced.
- Returned a full verified foreground shelf immediately, tracking pending work
  through waitUntil. Background harvesting still awaits its complete work.

## Evidence and limits

- 79 affected behavioral tests passed. All 49 unique Node commands from CI passed.
- Independent review cleared three reproduced P2 issues after failing-test fixes.
- Final warm test: six three-item rotations per station, 18 different episode
  selections each, zero repeats, zero exhaustion resets, three ready every time.
  Cartoons used eight series keys; TV used twelve catalog series keys (multiple
  Archive parents can belong to the same television show).
- Warm queue responses: cartoons 41–65 ms; TV 82–117 ms. These are API timings,
  not first visible-frame timings or promises for physical devices.
- Browser playback: Top Cat → Pink Panther Show and Bonanza → Dragnet had actual
  decoded landscape frames and advancing playback time, not merely metadata.
- Failed cold evidence is retained: the final cold attempt had an Archive
  preflight timeout; its retry timed out on cartoons and a later TV rotation.
  Warming recovered both stations. Cold-start resilience is not certified solved.
- Research counts are file-metadata depth, not a claim that every inspected file
  is licensed, transport-certified, or decoded on every device. This release
  does not declare a full IA, physical mobile, or Chromecast certification pass.

Local raw evidence remains under artifacts/ia-depth-* and ia-families-*.
The remaining suite rollout continues under the approved IA rollout plan.
