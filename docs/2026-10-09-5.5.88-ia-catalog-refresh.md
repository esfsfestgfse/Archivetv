# 5.5.88 — Keep production IA catalogs discovering

Production checks after 5.5.87 exposed a difference from the local canary:
the verified D1 fast path stopped background refresh at fifteen records.
Classic TV remained at 34 and cartoons at 47 in isolated production session
rotations even though the relay could build substantially deeper catalogs.

For repaired IA requests, the fast path now keeps importing qualified relay
discoveries on a bounded five-minute per-isolate/channel cadence. Foreground
tuning still returns its existing verified shelf immediately. Unverified or
empty refreshes are not imported. Same-sized refreshes can contain different
episodes and are merged through existing durable upserts rather than discarded.

Legacy, OK, Vimeo, FAST and Source Suite refresh behavior is unchanged. This is
a follow-up connection repair, not a new backend or broader channel enrollment.
The cadence is per isolate, not a guarantee of one global refresh per channel.

Two new failing behavioral tests passed after the fix: healthy 30-item D1
catalogs trigger non-blocking, coalesced discovery; equal-sized new catalogs
persist new identities. The affected shared suite now passes 81 tests.
The deeper 5.5.87 collection and rotation evidence remains in that release note.
Cold Archive timeouts and full-suite/device certification remain open work.
