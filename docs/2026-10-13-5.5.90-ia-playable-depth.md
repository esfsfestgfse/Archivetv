# 5.5.90 — IA playable depth and editorial identity

This release repairs three narrow failures found after the 5.5.89 catalog-breadth handoff:

- Background harvesting now measures verified playable depth instead of stopping when the metadata bank is full.
- Incomplete complete-series collections continue through a bounded background harvest even when the current candidate window is full.
- Classic Cartoons applies its canonical exclusion rules to D1 fallback rows, preventing reviews, reactions, podcasts, tutorials, and similar commentary from entering playback.
- Session balancing normalizes show-family punctuation, so identifiers such as `get-smart` and `Get Smart` cannot occupy separate family slots.

The public shelf remains small and fast; only background discovery and server-side admission changed. OK Movies and OK TV are unchanged in this release.
