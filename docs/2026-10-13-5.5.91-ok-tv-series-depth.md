# 5.5.91 — OK TV series depth and freshness

This release keeps the 5.5.90 Internet Archive repair intact and improves the
OK TV lane without changing OK Movies.

- Background discovery now inspects ten distinct English-language US series
  per pass instead of three.
- The verified D1 catalog remains a large 512-item candidate pool; only the
  small ready shelf is sent to playback.
- Server and client rotation now remember the previous ten shows separately
  from individual episode history.
- A different series is preferred for tune and Next whenever another verified
  show is available. Recently watched shows remain a fallback for constrained
  catalogs, preventing an empty shelf.
- Desktop and mobile use the same series-first selection and visible 5.5.91
  build stamp.

Regression coverage verifies ten-series discovery, catalogs deeper than 96
episodes, five-show shelves, a ten-show cooldown, and sixteen unseen rotations.
