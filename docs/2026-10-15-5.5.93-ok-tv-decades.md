# 5.5.93 — OK TV decade channels

Channels 600–602 are now part of the normal desktop and mobile lineup:

- 600 — OK TV: The 80s
- 601 — OK TV: The 90s
- 602 — OK TV: The 2000s

The channels are derived from the verified OK TV parent catalog using each
series' canonical premiere decade. They use independent viewer history, a
24-show cooldown, five ready programs, and exact-episode freshness. Exhausted
singleton shows cannot force a watched episode back onto the visible shelf.

Production certification measured 105, 197, and 192 playable episodes for the
three decades respectively, with full shelves, no wrong-decade rows, no
blocked formats, and no exact repeats across the tested rotations. The 1980s
lane remains the smallest catalog and should continue receiving background
depth harvests without delaying playback.
