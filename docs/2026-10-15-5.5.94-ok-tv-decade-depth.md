# 5.5.94 — OK TV decade depth

The verified OK TV parent catalog now indexes television across every requested
decade from the 1950s through the 2020s. New decade profiles remain hidden
server-side canaries until they independently satisfy the same depth,
freshness, and playback gates used for channels 600–602.

Deployment also repairs missing canonical TVmaze premiere metadata for legacy
OK TV records. This moves already-verified episodes into the correct decade
without changing their playback URLs or weakening the existing admission
rules.

Public channels 600–602 remain unchanged and continue using their independent
freshness histories. No shallow decade channel is exposed by this release.
