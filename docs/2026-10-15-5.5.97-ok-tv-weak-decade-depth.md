# 5.5.97 — OK TV weak-decade depth

Targeted background discovery now deliberately rotates through one canonical
1950s, 1960s, and 2020s television identity on every targeted maintenance
pass. These are discovery rails rather than admission whitelists: TVmaze still
verifies the actual series and premiere decade, while the shared runtime,
language, landscape, and playback gates decide whether an episode enters the
parent catalog.

Broad TVmaze indexing remains active for every decade. No hidden decade lane
is added to the public guide by this release.
