## Delivered scope

Its reviewed recovery/discovery rails grow from eight cartoon families and 168 file candidates to **30 families, 32 Archive parents, and 605 metadata-qualified full-program candidates**. Those counts describe the reviewed bank, not 605 simultaneously transport-verified or device-decoded videos. ## Why this is deeper

- Reviewed parent identifiers enter discovery independently of misleading upload years. Broader series vocabulary also reaches the normal collection search rail on desktop and mobile.
- Full episodes and legitimate anthology blocks qualify; individual short theatrical cartoons do not bypass the 15-minute floor.
- Existing background collection expansion, resumable discovery, freshness history, and the three-item ready shelf remain active. The reviewed bank is not a fixed five-video playlist.
- Multiple editions of Tom and Jerry and Atom Ant share series identity. Redundant numbered Josie derivatives share logical episode identity without altering their media URLs. Six redundant Fat Albert alternate/study cuts are excluded during discovery and when stored records are read back.
- Stock animated-background loops and explicit Portuguese-dub markers are rejected on this station. Landscape, runtime, fan/parody/podcast, and other genre checks remain in force.

The final run reached 67 admitted catalog records; queue response median was 542 ms, cold response 1,282 ms. This is transport/rotation evidence, not a first-visible-frame benchmark.

Classic TV and both game-show era canaries also passed targeted checks. All 131 focused regression tests and all 53 CI-equivalent Node commands passed before shipping.

This release does not certify all IA channels or physical mobile/Cast hardware. Archive availability, CDN assignments, and qualifying inventory remain variable. Some metadata has no language field; explicit foreign-edition signals are rejected, but missing language metadata is not proof of English audio. Broader content/audio audits remain separate from successful transport checks.
