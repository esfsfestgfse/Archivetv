# RealSignal 5.6.19 — Modern Game Shows and actual-play freshness

## Delivered scope

- Modern Game Shows remains channel 22, covering 1980–present. Classic Game Shows remains channel 12, covering television's beginnings through 1979.
- Removed Nickelodeon and other children's game-show identities from discovery, API/file admission, and desktop/mobile saved/direct playback. Subject-only and possessive “Kids' Week” labels are covered. Family Feud and adult/general-audience editions remain eligible.
- Versioned the old standard channel-22 session catalog so it cannot reintroduce removed programs. Viewer history is retained through the separate played-history input.
- Added full, dated Archive episode-file indexes for The Price Is Right, Wheel of Fortune and Jeopardy!, rather than relying only on collection titles and keyword discovery.
- Background transport qualification tries different shows before working through a large single-series block. It retains the complete candidate union and existing batch/concurrency limits; it adds no foreground discovery requirement.
- Show-identity admission rejects loose keyword matches, unrelated news/drama/documentaries, mixed soap blocks and identified non-English editions. The tested rejected examples include a concentration-camp documentary, a Sidekicks drama episode, a political headline mentioning “deal or no deal,” and Philippine Family Feud recordings. Channel 22's relay/session policy version advances independently; other channels are not flushed.
- Standard IA rotation now counts actual played/skipped history, not every buffered shelf item. History matches alternate encodings and resolves a reported legacy parent alias to the corresponding file without banning unseen sibling episodes. Source Suite and guarded IA reserve/commit ownership remain unchanged.

These are catalog records, not 398 decoded-video tests.

- 44 sampled real Archive media Range requests: 44 passed.
- Cold local game-show rotation after background balancing: 24 selections, 24 unique, no queue failures; the first three selections were Jeopardy, Price and Wheel. Loopback has no production D1 cache; its worst request took 11.622 seconds, so this is not an instant-start claim.
- 131 focused behavioral tests passed, including actual hosted genre-contamination examples. All 79 commands from the CI workflow passed locally after the final show-identity fix.
- Each new regression was observed failing before its corresponding repair.
- A bounded independent read-only review found three important cases; each received a reproducing regression test and fix. The later hosted parent/file-alias case received an additional failing-then-passing test.

## Boundaries

No channels were hidden or removed. No channel numbers were reassigned. OK, Vimeo, FAST, Roku and Source Suite were not redesigned. Existing untracked audits and research files were left untouched. This release is a targeted genre/depth/freshness repair, not a claim that all IA content is genre-audited or that an upstream provider can never fail.
