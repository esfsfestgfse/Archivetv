# RealSignal 5.6.20 — whole-lineup IA audit and first repair

## Scope and evidence

Every current canonical IA channel was included: **170 active identities**, plus **13 explicit legacy aliases** that are not counted twice. No channels were added, hidden, merged or renumbered. OK, Vimeo, FAST and paused Roku work were not changed.

The production 5.6.19 baseline completed **2,550 selections** (15 per channel), including actual skip acknowledgements, with **zero queue/skip endpoint errors**. This proves selection availability, not 170-channel visible-video certification.

All 170 catalogs were separately captured. The complete coverage tables and JSON preserve measured file counts, metadata-qualified hours, declared families/decades, duplicate-title suspects, rotation findings and unknowns. Counts describe returned catalog windows, **not the entire Archive inventory**. Unknown geometry or duration is not evidence of bad content. A playable URL is not transport proof, and transport proof is not a decoded frame.

- `artifacts/ia-coverage-5620-before.json` and `.md`

## Confirmed shared defect and repair

Fresh discovery had content-policy gates, but ordinary D1 catalog admission, old durable rotation catalogs and desktop/mobile saved playback could bypass them. Known podcasts, fan-made movies and portrait/Shorts uploads could therefore survive an older cache.

A narrow synchronous policy gate now checks those paths. It does not demand missing metadata, impose a new blanket runtime floor or run discovery during tuning. Original short cartoons, ordinary studio satire and radio's separate audio contract remain allowed. The server/client implementations have explicit parity tests. Existing seen history and session ownership are preserved; forbidden rows are filtered from durable catalogs without resetting the viewer's rotation.

Applying the new gate to the complete captured catalog set rejects **42 known-bad candidates across 17 channels**, while retaining a nonempty catalog in every channel. This is an offline comparison, not a claim that 42 database rows were deleted or that every genre problem is solved.
