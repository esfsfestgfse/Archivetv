# RealSignal 5.6.21 — preserve legitimate movie titles

This is the final narrow correction to the whole-lineup repair documented in `2026-10-20-5.6.20-ia-lineup-repair.md`. No catalog architecture, station identity, rotation history or source provider was changed.

## Correction

The new known-only cache gate initially treated any occurrence of “portrait” or “vertical” as orientation evidence. A failing regression test demonstrated that legitimate landscape movie titles could be excluded. Admission now uses actual geometry or explicit format declarations such as “portrait video,” “vertical format,” “filmed in portrait,” `9:16` and `#shorts`. Ordinary titles such as *Portrait of Jennie* and *Vertical Limit* do not assert a video's orientation. Server, desktop and mobile remain behaviorally aligned.

The captured baseline contained one concrete false positive: channel 501's *Portrait of an Artist: Stephen Curry — Official Trailer*, a legitimate item for Trailer Park. The final gate therefore rejects **41 candidates across 16 channels**, rather than calling all 42 original matches violations. Unknown metadata remains unknown; genuine portrait geometry, podcasts and fan productions remain excluded.
