# RealSignal 5.6.22 — expanded IA file depth

This is a targeted repair of four measured shallow stations, not a new channel architecture or a claim that the whole IA lineup is finished. Channel numbers, visibility, other providers, and the existing rolling playback shelf remain unchanged.

## What changed

| Station | Previously observed exhaustion | Added reviewed file records | Programming |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 20 Courtroom TV | 12 selections | 426 | Judge Judy, Judge Joe Brown, Judge Mathis, The People's Court, Divorce Court |
| 65 The Sporting Life | 14 selections, including contamination | 23 | Fishing, hunting and outdoor television/VHS programs |
| 204 Prelinger Vault | 12 selections | 33 | Complete educational, industrial and sponsored films |
| 510 The Game Room | 14 selections | 233 | GamesMaster, G4 Icons, Retro Core and Bad Influence! |

The 715 new records retain their actual Archive parent, file, media URL, metadata runtime, landscape dimensions and source rights text. They enter the existing server-side recovery/catalog path. They are not 715 videos simultaneously downloaded by the browser. A small immediate shelf remains in front of a larger catalog, with bounded background verification.

Collections are interleaved by series, not merely by season/upload container. Different encodes and identified alternate copies of the same episode are removed. A single-program parent and its matching `parent::parent.mp4` now share a relay freshness identity; different episode files remain separate.

Courtroom TV now requires a judge-show identity rather than accepting news descriptions that mention judges. Sporting Life rejects civic meetings, concerts, ambiguous comedy/fiction matches, ad-break compilations and the observed `HuntingSeason` scavenger-hunt film. The API, persisted session catalogs and desktop/mobile saved-playback paths apply these station rules. Existing real viewing history is retained.

Only the four repaired relay shelf cache identities receive the new depth-policy revision. No viewer ledger, database or channel is deleted. There are no schema, secret, dependency or binding changes and no additional API routing rewrite.

## Research and admission evidence

- 269 Archive metadata inspections.
- 884 candidate files received bounded media probes: 747 passed, 15 failed, and 122 were held after repeated parent timeouts.
- Only successful probes were eligible for the generated bank. Duplicate removal reduced those results to 715 records.
- New files require at least 900 seconds and landscape geometry. Existing reviewed short-cartoon and radio policies are unchanged.
- A successful video byte-range response proves transport at the observation time, not permanent availability, decoded playback of every file, English-audio certification, or licensing clearance. Rights fields are provenance, not a clearance assertion.

## Reproduction and verification

Behavioral tests reproduce and prevent station contamination, saved mobile cache bypasses, parent/file replay, missing file-bank admission and same-series opening shelves. Empty queue/cache entries remain ordinary misses rather than exceptions.

The final four-lane loopback API/relay test completed 24 actual selection/skip acknowledgements per channel: **96 selections, 96 unique, zero errors**. An additional 48-selection run on each of Courtroom and Game Room completed **96 selections, 96 unique, zero errors** after fixing the reproduced parent/file aliases. The first longer run did expose two aliases; it is retained as failure evidence, not counted as a pass.

Browser observations showed decoded landscape video on all four channels: Courtroom 720×535/640×480, Sporting Life 640×480, Prelinger 640×480 and Game Room 640×480. Courtroom Next changed from Judge Joe Brown to Judge Judy with advancing video. Guide open/close and card tuning responded. These are bounded desktop observations, not every-file playback certification or a timed first-frame benchmark. Physical mobile/Cast testing was not performed.

The guide's text search returned zero matches for “Courtroom” during this check even while its cleared guide card was present and tunable. No guide implementation changed in this release; that observed search defect remains a separate follow-up, not a claimed pass.

The complete 80-command CI workflow passed locally on the final candidate. After the normal push, verify all five GitHub workflows (CI, relay, API, publish and Pages), public desktop/mobile/API stamps, and fresh hosted rotations before reporting production success. Raw research and playback traces remain local audit artifacts; they are not bundled into the application.

## Remaining work and rollback

Sporting Life and Prelinger now have deeper clean banks, but remain much smaller than Courtroom/Game Room. These counts are not endless catalogs. Judge Judy still accounts for much of Courtroom's total inventory; opening-series balance does not invent additional series after smaller pools are exhausted.

The previous whole-lineup capture covers 170 canonical IA stations plus 13 aliases. This release does not relabel that earlier coverage as a new full-lineup playback soak. Remaining recorded long-tail findings still need targeted depth/genre repairs. Next prioritize those measured findings and the observed guide search issue, without another broad rewrite.

Rollback is the previous main release commit `04836509693a09b8d4fe7027990fc35576c5bcc4` through the normal revert/deployment workflow. No destructive state reset or data migration is needed.
