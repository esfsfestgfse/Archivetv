# RealSignal 5.6.23 — food, factories and guide search

This is a targeted repair of measured shallow IA lanes, not another backend rewrite or a claim that every station is finished. Channel numbers, visibility, other providers and the small rolling playback shelf remain unchanged.

## What changed

| Station | Before: 24 queue selections | Added reviewed file records | Programming |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 200 Manufacturing Marvels | 15 unique, 9 repeated | 25 | Inside the Factory, Factory Made, How It's Made, automotive production and complete industrial films |
| 201 Cooking & Food | 19 unique, 5 repeated; etiquette/community contamination | 349 | The French Chef, Yan Can Cook, America's Test Kitchen, Good Eats, Great Chefs, Julia Child and Jacques Pepin programs |

The 374 records retain exact Archive parent/file identities, observed media URLs, metadata runtime and landscape geometry. They feed the existing server-side catalog path, not a 374-video browser download. Cooking has 28 reviewed parent containers; Manufacturing has 25. Individual file records can include complete compilation/disc programs as well as individual episodes. These are not 374 independently cleared titles.

Cooking now requires a cooking-program identity rather than incidental food subject tags. Office etiquette, civic broadcasts and the observed “Building the Recipe View/Model” software lessons are rejected at relay, API, persisted session and desktop/mobile cache admission. Legitimate culinary instruction remains allowed. Test Kitchen is explicitly recognized even when the filename lacks “food” or “cooking.”

Cooking alone no longer uses the D1 fast-shelf shortcut that could mask its deeper relay bank. Its existing generic session-rotation owner and viewing history are preserved. Manufacturing's title vocabulary now admits the reviewed factory/process titles in both clients without removing its existing contamination exclusions. The browser-observed industrial-mural art footage is excluded through the shared station manifest. Three alternate factory encodes were removed rather than counted as fresh programs.

Guide search now includes the canonical displayed channel label. Searches for “Courtroom TV,” “Cooking & Food” and other merged labels return their one existing card; legacy names and numeric searches remain supported. There is no renumbering or visibility change.

Only 200/201 receive this release's depth-cache revision. The four 5.6.22 cache identities are unchanged. No database migration, binding, secret, dependency or ledger reset is required.
