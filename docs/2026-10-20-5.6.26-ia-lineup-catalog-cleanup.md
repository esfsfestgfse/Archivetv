# RealSignal 5.6.26 — IA catalog cleanup

The remaining 43 entries were audit/review flags, not 43 dead channels. A fresh production capture returned nonempty queues for all 43 with no request errors. Thirty-nine catalog windows still contained records rejected by the file contract: known short runtimes, non-English editions, explicit fan reconstructions and portrait geometry. Some flags were false positives: a landscape episode lived inside a collection folder named Shorts, and legitimate studio satire was classified as fan-made parody.

## Shared repair

The existing synchronous known-bad admission check now also receives the station identity. It applies known selected-file runtimes and declared language, not guessed parent runtime or missing metadata. This gate runs on relay candidates, API/D1 reads, saved durable rotation and desktop/mobile queue/cache admission. Existing movie and ordinary-TV floors are respected; reviewed original cartoons, audio, short-subject stations and intentionally international stations retain their exceptions. Regional English codes are accepted; undetermined language is not proof of a foreign edition.

Explicit fanedit/fanfic/reaction uploads remain rejected. A bare word such as Portrait in a movie title, Shorts in a collection directory, or parody describing a studio comedy no longer establishes forbidden orientation or fan authorship. Actual geometry and explicit format declarations still reject portrait uploads. No metadata-only row is relabelled as transport-verified or decoded playback.

## Three measured follow-ups

- Tight Lines: 12 reviewed long-form fishing records across 12 Archive parents, including In-Fisherman, Bill Dance and Babe Winkelman. Ninety Adventure Bound candidates were held after review found matching DVD continuation chunks: a first chunk is not a complete episode. The known chunked source is excluded from active shelves until complete-file or continuation support can be verified. Personal home movies, civic/community matches, aquaculture and the observed declared-Japanese Daiwa edition are excluded from this station. Other mixed collections are not banned wholesale.
- Britain on Film: 10 reviewed full-program documentary/travel records. A Hitchcock fiction film and observed personal-travel reels no longer substitute for the station's factual programming. Opaque DVD chapter extras, duplicate North Wales uploads and partial films were held out; one full British Transport Films DVD program is retained.
- Range & Field: three additional full-length bowhunting/archery programs. This remains a constrained pool, not an endless catalog. Short promos, unrelated material and non-English editions are not added merely to inflate depth. Its old D1 shortcut is bypassed only after a reproducing routing test showed it could hide the new file bank.

All 115 candidates passed bounded video-range checks, but 90 incomplete DVD-title candidates were held out. The final bank has 25 retained records (12 fishing, three archery, 10 British film). Rights text and source provenance are retained; those checks do not establish licensing clearance, permanent availability, audio-language validation or successful decoding of every file. Review also reproduced and repaired raw `mediatype:audio` discovery rejection, inappropriate one-hour floors on mixed holiday/silent/festival formats, and selected-file metadata nested inside saved client records. Every Important finding had a failing-then-passing behavioral reproduction. The new records use the existing rolling shelf and state owner, not a new queue system. Played history is retained. Only the three new bank cache revisions change; previous repair revisions remain intact.

Release tests, browser observations and fresh hosted re-audit results are recorded with their precise limits in the progress ledger. No channels are hidden, removed or renumbered.
