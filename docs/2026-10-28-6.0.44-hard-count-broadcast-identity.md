# RealSignal 6.0.44: football identity for Archive broadcasts

The targeted 6.0.43 production rotation exposed a remaining bad head:
`MSNBCW_20120919_093000_Way_Too_Early_With_Willie_Geist`. A finite list of
newscast names was insufficient, so that run was not treated as certification.

For channel 51, station/date/time Archive recording IDs now require an actual
football-program identity in their programme slug (or an NFL station). Subject
tags and a rewritten display title cannot qualify a generic news recording.
Known general-newscast and wrestling format exclusions remain in force.
Ordinary uploads are unaffected by this broadcast-specific rule.

The same admission boundary is used by the module/API, relay, desktop and
mobile, including stored shelves and pre-Cast publication. Tests reproduce
the MSNBC failure, reject a misleading CNN display title and preserve an NFL
football broadcast plus NFL Network's America's Game. Other channel numbers
retain their own programming. No history or catalogs were erased.

149 affected tests pass. Production queue and browser playback evidence is
saved separately after deployment; a media URL is not a visible-frame result.
This remains a single-lane admission/recovery repair, not a full IA audit.
