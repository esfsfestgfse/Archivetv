# RealSignal 6.0.24 — One Benson series identity across catalog generations

The extended hosted test found that legacy titles beginning Benson and refreshed titles beginning Benson Complete Series were mapped to different family identities. Their Archive parent and series were identical. This let two Benson episodes occupy a shelf whose family cap was one.

Benson now resolves to one canonical television-show identity when the record has no conflicting editorial series. An explicitly different series remains authoritative; a stray title mention cannot overwrite it. The regression uses the two actual production label forms and first reproduced two Benson slots instead of one. The corrected reservation fills the other slots with eligible Beaver and Munsters episodes.

This release retains the 6.0.22 faster imports for shallow Classic TV and commentary-admission repair, and the 6.0.23 two-pass priority balancing. Catalog inventory, watched history and promised reservations are preserved. Hosted first-frame, rotation and guide checks follow deployment; no whole-lineup or physical mobile/Cast certification is claimed.
