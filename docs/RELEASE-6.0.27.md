# RealSignal 6.0.27 — Saturday Morning television-format admission

Follow-up public queue testing of 6.0.26 exposed two amateur/community puppet recordings newly visible in the balanced IA window: a church performance and a community-center Hajj performance. Archive metadata confirms these are performances rather than television programs.

Saturday Morning now requires television-program evidence when a generic puppet-show recording is proposed. Familiar children’s TV, including Sesame Street, Muppets and Colby’s Clubhouse, remains eligible; unfamiliar series can qualify through explicit television metadata. The restriction applies to Saturday Morning only, preserving those performances on appropriate cultural channels. All four shared admission copies use the same rule, including persisted catalog reads.

The actual leaks and positive television examples first reproduced in the shared regression test, then passed after the rule change. This release retains the balanced catalog window and the reviewed identity/genre repairs shipped in 6.0.25–6.0.26. No catalog, viewing history, channel identity or reservation was deleted. Full offline checks and targeted hosted playback/guide verification are required before completion; they do not certify every channel on every device.
