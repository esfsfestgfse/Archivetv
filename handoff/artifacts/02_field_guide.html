What Afterglow Is

A television for a signal that stopped broadcasting a while ago.

Afterglow is a retro-CRT web app that channel-surfs across the Internet Archive, NASA, NOAA, USGS, the LOC National Jukebox, Wikimedia EventStreams, ADS-B feeds, ERCOT, and about a dozen more public sources. It runs entirely in your browser — no account, no subscription, no ads — and installs as a PWA on Android or as a bookmarked page anywhere else.

The name is what a phosphor screen does after you cut the power: the picture doesn't leave immediately. That's what late-night TV feels like, and that's what this app is trying to be.

The Remote Control

Every control lives in one panel on the right edge of the screen.

Tap the right edge of the tube to summon the remote. It fades on its own after a few seconds; tap the picture to dismiss it immediately.

FAV ★
  • PowerWakes the tube. The screen warms up in about half a second — CRT ident, snow, then the picture. Hit power again to send the whole thing back through the vintage sign-off sequence.
  • CH ▲ / ▼ · Number entrySurf one channel at a time, or type a three-digit number to jump directly. Dead channels get auto-skipped so you're never left staring at a blank.
  • GuideOpens the Prevue-Guide-style program grid: preview pane on top, scrolling ticker at the bottom, sections divided by category. Live Data lives in its own amber section at the bottom.
  • Menu · InfoMENU opens the on-screen display with 22 items grouped into Picture, While Watching, This Channel, Library, and System sections. INFO shows what's on now, what's on next, and where the current item came from.
  • Skip · Save · FavSKIP jumps to the next item in the current channel. SAVE tapes what you're watching to your Tape Shelf. FAV ★ toggles a favorite (later reachable via FAVORITE SURF in the OSD).

Hidden Features

Things not on the remote.

The interface tries to look like a TV, so a lot of the power is behind a menu button. Here's what's worth finding.

Phosphor Tint

MENU → PICTURE & VIEW → PHOSPHOR TINT cycles the CRT through Green (P1 default) → Amber (P3) → Cyan (P4) → White (mono). The chrome stays green; only the tube shifts. Persists across sessions.

CRT Effects

Press C anywhere to cycle CRT scanline intensity: OFF · SUBTLE · MEDIUM · FULL. Higher levels add scan-lines, chromatic aberration, and static.

Favorite Surf

Star a channel with FAV ★, then MENU → FAVORITE SURF plays only from your starred set. Great for building a personal 10-channel dial inside the 147.

Deep Cuts

MENU → DEEP CUTS makes the picker prefer older + more obscure items over the popular ones. On some channels the difference is dramatic.

Marathon Lock

MENU → MARATHON LOCK freezes the current channel to skip only within its own catalog when SKIP is pressed — no automatic time-of-day slot changes.

EAS Test

MENU → EAS TEST triggers the full Emergency Alert takeover sequence for your county. Sound + visual + duck-tone. Best experienced with the volume up.

Sleep Timer

MENU → SLEEP TIMER cycles OFF/5/15/30/60/90/120 min. Ends with the vintage sign-off sequence (fade + CRT shrink + color bars).

Developer Mode

Tap the build-number chip beside STANDBY five times rapidly to unlock the DEVELOPER drawer in the OSD — Soak Mode, Soak Report, Reset Dead-Channel List, Sports Era override.

Under the Hood

Where the signal comes from.

Everything Afterglow shows comes from a public source. Nothing here is scraped from a subscription service, nothing here is stolen. The list is long because a good dial has many suppliers.
