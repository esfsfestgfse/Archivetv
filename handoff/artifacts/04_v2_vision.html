Afterglow · roadmap

The dial stops being a television and starts being an instrument.

v1 asked "what if the Internet Archive had a channel dial?" We answered with 148 channels, a live-data spine that pulls from 25 government feeds, and a rebrand. v2 asks a different question: given that the picture works, what should the picture do for you that only a real-time channelized surface can?

v2 · The Thesis

Four pillars. All of them make the app work harder for the viewer, not the other way around.

v1 was about coverage — how many channels can we curate, how many feeds can we plumb. v2 is about use — how does the app anticipate what you need, how does it split its attention across multiple channels at once, how does it let you build your own dial. Pillars are designed to be shippable independently; you can pick any one and it stands on its own.

01

Pillar 01

Storm Mode

The dial becomes a chase companion. When SPC issues a moderate risk, NHC posts a new tropical advisory, USGS registers a >M5.5 near a populated area, or NWS drops a tornado warning for your county — the app auto-tunes to the most relevant data channel, holds it, and shows the alerting summary as a persistent bug. Nothing to click; the picture already knows.

Alert bus

Poll SPC/NHC/USGS/NWS every 60 sec, dedup by event ID, normalize to a single event stream.

Tentpole

Auto-tune

On a new event above a user-set severity, hold current, snap to Local Radar / Storm Center / Tropical Watch, resume after N min.

Tentpole

Multi-alert queue

Two active events → split the queue: rotate between the two channels every 20 sec until either clears.

Chase-day audio bed

When Storm Mode is active, swap the country/ragtime beds for a subtle procedural drone. Ambient anxiety.

Chaser log

Every tune during an active event auto-logs to a Chase Sheet: time, event, channel, location. Export as CSV or share sheet.

Post-storm replay

After the event closes, offer to replay the last 24 hours of your Chase Sheet as a marathon.

02

Pillar 02

Multi-View

Split the tube. Watch a chase primary + Local Radar sidebar + Storm Center bottom bar + Sky Beacon corner simultaneously, from one Set. Or Sports Vault primary + live scoreboard ticker + injury feed corner. The dial hasn't been able to be more than one channel at once for its whole life; that's the whole product.

2-up / 3-up / quad layouts

Preset splits — 2-up (50/50), 3-up (1 primary + 2 stacked), quad (2×2). Each pane is its own tuned channel.

Tentpole

Overlay mode

A data channel (Local Radar, Kp index) semi-transparently overlaid on a video channel. Corner-anchored.

Audio focus

Only one pane has audio. Tap another pane to swap focus. Others stay muted.

Layout memory

Every layout saveable + nameable ("Storm Watch", "Sunday Football"). Recall from OSD.

Auto-multi triggers

Certain events auto-switch to a specific layout — e.g. a tornado warning triggers "Storm Watch" (Radar + Storm Center + LOCAL ALERT bug).

03

Pillar 03

Build Your Own Channels

Turn 148 curated channels into infinite user channels. A CHANNEL FORGE screen lets you compose a channel from Internet Archive search terms + era range + subject filters + collection allow-list. Save it. Give it a number. It shows up in the guide with your name on the ident. Optionally share via URL so friends can subscribe.

Forge UI

One-screen wizard: name, category, era slider, IA collection multi-select, subject include/exclude, deny-title regex.

Tentpole

Live preview

As you type filters, the wizard shows the next 5 items that would play. Reject → refine.

User channel numbers

User channels reserve num 601–699 (their own neighborhood in the guide). Named category "MY CHANNELS."

Share by URL

Every user channel encodes as a share URL. Import lands as ch 601+ next available slot with the source user's name in the sub-line.

Community remix

Optional community gallery of published user channels. Star, fork, adopt. No hosting cost — everything's a URL.

04

Pillar 04

The Time Machine

Set the date. Not the calendar date — the watch date. Move the dial back to July 20, 1969 and the schedule adapts: Live News becomes Moon-landing wire coverage, Newsstand shows that day's front pages, Storm Center shows the actual weather map from that day, On This Day surfaces the events. Move to April 15, 1912 → Titanic front pages, Wax Museum shows recordings from that year. It's the archive as a time-travel apparatus, not a search box.

Time-slider control

A vintage rotary date-picker in the OSD. Snap to significant days (moon landing, JFK, V-J, Katrina, 9/11). Or free-scrub.

Tentpole

Date-locked programming

Every video-content channel filters PROGRAM catalog to items published within ±1 year of the selected date.

Tentpole

Historical Newsstand

Chronicling America already scopes by date — feed the selected date directly. Front pages match.

Historical Storm Center

SPC/NHC don't publish historical products via API, but NOAA has PDF archives — surface the best-available for the date.

Historical Wax Museum

LOC National Jukebox filters by year already — bind to the date directly.

Storm chase replay

Feed a specific historic storm date (Katrina Aug 29 2005, Harvey Aug 26 2017) and the whole app becomes that day's dashboard.

For you

How we ship v2 · phases

Each pillar rolls out on its own runway.

No mega-release. Every pillar is independently valuable and shippable. Phase them by dependency: infrastructure first (alert bus + user-channel storage schema), then the visible surfaces on top.

Foundation · 1-2 weeks

Alert bus + user-channel schema

Two invisible pieces of infrastructure that everything else stands on. The alert-poller (SPC/NHC/USGS/NWS every 60 sec, event dedup, severity classification) is a prerequisite for Storm Mode and for Multi-View's auto-triggers. The user-channel storage schema (localStorage shape + URL encoding scheme) is the prerequisite for Build Your Own Channels.

Pillar 01 · 1 week

Storm Mode

Ship on top of the alert bus. Auto-tune + persistent alert bug + Chase Sheet in one release. Storm Mode is the fastest full pillar to ship and the most immediately useful — puts real value in front of the storm-season user right away.

Pillar 02 · 2 weeks

Multi-View

Layout system + audio focus + preset library. Riskiest of the four (touches every tuner + the video/iframe management) but no external dependencies. Auto-multi triggers wire in for free from the alert bus already built in the Foundation phase.

Pillar 03 · 2 weeks

Build Your Own Channels

Forge UI + live preview + user-channel neighborhood in the guide (601-699). Share-by-URL and community remix land in a second sub-phase after the base flow works.

Pillar 04 · 2-3 weeks

The Time Machine

Most engineering-heavy — requires touching PROGRAM catalog filtering, adding historical-newsstand mode to Newsstand, date-scoped Wax Museum. Ship the time-slider + date-locked programming first; historical-Storm-Center and storm-replay follow in later releases as best-available data becomes accessible.

Explicit non-goals

What v2 is not.

To keep the roadmap honest, an equal list of things we're not building. Each of these has been considered and rejected on purpose.

Native app / Electron wrapper

Would let us cast to Chromecast + install as a desktop app. Deferred. The single-file HTML PWA constraint is load-bearing to how fast the app iterates. If a native shell is worth it, that's a v3 conversation, not a v2 one.

Social layer / watch parties

Sync tuning across friends, comment tickers, "now watching" ambient list. Deferred. Interesting but pulls the app toward being a chat product; we want it to stay a tuning surface.

Podcast integration

Turning podcast RSS feeds into audio channels. Deferred. Adjacent to the archive theme but Podcast RSS-to-play is a fully different content pipeline; save for later.

Full account system

User accounts, cross-device sync, cloud favorites. Deferred. localStorage + URL-shared user channels covers 95% of the value at 5% of the infrastructure cost.

Payment / donation flow

Not planned. The whole point is a free channel-surfing surface on top of free public archives. Introducing money changes the contract.

An "AI Curator" chat

An LLM that recommends what to watch. Not planned. The whole product is that we've curated 148 channels; a chatbot layer between viewer and content would undermine the tuning ritual that IS the app.

Closing note

v1 built the television. v2 builds the reason to keep it on.

The dial has always worked as a spinning-of-the-knob toy — pleasant, occasionally revelatory. What Storm Mode + Multi-View + user channels + the Time Machine do is give the viewer something the dial can do for them even when they're not spinning: watch the sky for them, split its attention across four channels for them, be their channel that only they care about, or move them backward through time on demand. That's when the app stops being novelty and starts being something they leave open.

Every pillar independently valuable. Every pillar shippable in a small number of weeks. Every pillar rooted in what v1 already knows how to do — nothing here requires new infrastructure that v1 didn't already prove out. That's the roadmap.
